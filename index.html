My 6-Step Design Process

The same process behind every static asset and every reel I edit — from reading the real world to shipping the design.

01
Observe
Weather, culture, trends, events, market & environment.
02
Understand
Audience behavior, needs, emotions & business context.
03
Predict

04
Strategize

05
Design

06
Measure & Evolve
